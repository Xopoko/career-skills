# Career Market Research

Answer a bounded career decision with current evidence. This skill researches
the market; `opportunity-analysis` maps findings to one user.

Read:

- `../../references/market-and-opportunity-research.md`
- `../../references/research-evidence.md` for safe use of taxonomies and pay
  research
- `../../references/safety-privacy-fairness.md` for high-stakes questions

## Procedure

1. State the question, geography, role family, work arrangement, timeframe, and
   freshness need.
2. Identify which facts are live and must be verified now.
3. Reuse dated receipts and known URLs only as discovery leads under a declared
   freshness policy. Re-confirm every material employer claim before it shapes
   a current decision, application artifact, or interview brief.
4. Prefer canonical employer, government, regulator, taxonomy, filing, and
   current documentation sources; add independent evidence where it changes the
   decision.
5. Record publication date, represented date, retrieval time, geography,
   source type, and limitations.
6. Separate source assertion, independent observation, inference, and unknown.
7. Normalize compensation only after preserving raw amount, period, currency,
   location, level, employment basis, and sample context.
8. Preserve contradictions and missing coverage. Do not average incompatible
   sources.
9. For legal, tax, immigration, benefits, or labor-rights questions, use current
   authoritative sources and stop short of a legal conclusion.
10. Translate findings into a decision implication, cheapest verification step,
   and any search-policy change.

## Employer Research

Investigate only what affects the user's decision: role mandate, business and
financial signals, recent strategy, hiring state, team context, stability,
material controversies, and open questions. Treat employer statements,
independent reporting, and anecdotes as different evidence classes.

## Taxonomies

O*NET and ESCO can supply role language, tasks, and skill relationships. Persist
release or version, identifier, language, scale, and crosswalk relation. Never
treat an occupation average as a vacancy requirement or candidate fact.

## Output

Return a dated evidence table, bounded coverage, contradictions, decisive
unknowns, limitations, and what the evidence changes. Link sources near each
claim. Do not report stale market data as current.
