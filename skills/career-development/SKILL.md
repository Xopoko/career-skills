# Career Development

Turn a verified target or repeated pipeline gap into a bounded capability and
proof plan. Course completion alone is not the outcome.

Read:

- `../../references/career-direction-and-development.md`
- `../../references/pipeline-and-learning.md`
- `../../references/evidence-contract.md`

## Preconditions

Establish the target role or capability, baseline evidence, repeated gap signal,
time and budget ceiling, and the next decision the work should unlock. If the
user has no target hypothesis, route first to `career-direction`.

## Procedure

1. Validate the gap. Distinguish knowledge, practice, proof, signal, credential,
   eligibility, and preference.
2. Check whether a cheaper translation or evidence-retrieval step resolves it.
3. Research current formal requirements only when a credential or jurisdiction
   matters.
4. Define a capability outcome observable in realistic work.
5. Choose a practice task, feedback source, and proof artifact.
6. Add visibility or relationship work only when it serves the target audience.
7. Compare learning resources by task coverage, quality evidence, time, cost,
   accessibility, recency, and completion risk. Do not rank by popularity alone.
8. Build a milestone plan with effort ceiling, recovery rule, checkpoints,
   budget, and external effect gates.
9. Define abandon, adjust, and continue criteria before starting.
10. At each checkpoint, capture evidence and update facts only after validation.

## Internal Mobility And Growth

For promotion, scope expansion, mentoring, or internal transition, map current
expectations, decision makers, observable evidence, feedback, role openings, and
timing. Draft a development or scope conversation from supported examples.
Do not assume internal policy or promotion criteria; verify current sources.

## Output

Provide target capability, baseline, classified gap, chosen experiment, practice
and feedback loop, proof artifact, milestones, effort and budget caps, stop
conditions, and next career decision. Route purchases, enrollment, publishing,
or outbound requests through explicit effect plans.
