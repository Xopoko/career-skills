# Career Networking

Create a relevant human path with a small respectful ask. Do not automate mass
outreach or imply a relationship that does not exist.

Read:

- `../../references/networking-and-coordination.md`
- `../../references/safety-privacy-fairness.md`
- the effect rules in `../../references/operating-contract.md`

## Procedure

1. Define the goal: learn, validate fit, seek feedback, request an introduction,
   or ask for a referral.
2. Load the user's authorized relationship sources and privacy policy. Search no
   private network or contact source without permission.
3. Identify possible paths and why each is relevant.
4. Verify current affiliation and role from a current source.
5. Rank by genuine relationship strength, relevance, likely burden, and privacy;
   never by protected traits.
6. Choose the smallest appropriate ask from context question through referral.
7. Select one or two supported proof points relevant to the recipient.
8. Draft a concise message with real context, clear ask, easy decline, and no
   invented familiarity.
9. Review recipient, account, channel, full body, attachments, data disclosed,
   and timing.
10. Create a communication effect plan and stop before sending.

## Referral Boundary

Ask for a referral only when the recipient can reasonably assess the user's
work or the message gives enough evidence to decide. Do not pressure a weak tie,
promise reciprocity, or conceal that the user is seeking a referral.

## Contact Data

Retain only the source, current relevance, relationship basis, preferred
channel, consent or opt-out state, and follow-up decision needed. Do not build a
shadow dossier or copy unrelated personal content.

## Output

Return a ranked path map with verification dates, recommended ask, grounded
drafts, evidence used, privacy boundary, and effect plan if sending is requested.
Route inbound recruiter conversations to `recruiter-coordination`.
