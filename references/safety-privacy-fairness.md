# Safety, Privacy, Fairness, And Escalation

This reference supplies operational safeguards, not legal advice or a claim of
regulatory compliance.

## Scam And Exploitation Gate

Stop before the user:

- pays a purported employer, recruiter, or designated vendor for access to
  work, required equipment, training, onboarding, or release of pay as a
  condition of employment;
- deposits a check and forwards part of the funds;
- transfers cryptocurrency, buys gift cards, moves money, or performs paid
  rating tasks;
- receives, repackages, or reships goods;
- shares banking data, identity documents, or tax identifiers before the
  opening and recipient are independently verified;
- installs unknown software or grants remote-device access as part of an
  informal hiring exchange.

Record each observed signal and an independent verification step. Prefer the
organization's canonical domain, independently found contact details, and the
canonical vacancy page. A single odd phrase, free-mail address, urgency signal,
or missing page is a warning, not proof of fraud.

See the FTC job-scam guidance:
<https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/job-scams>.

## Sensitive And Protected Data

Never infer or optimize on race, color, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex,
pregnancy, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, disability, genetic or
health information, family status, or close proxies. A proxy is a variable used
to infer a protected trait, or as a substitute for that trait. Explicit,
job-related location, work-authorization, schedule, clearance, and language
requirements may be evaluated as stated constraints; never repurpose them to
infer protected identity. Do not include protected fields or proxies
in fit scoring, ranking, outreach targeting, or default retention.

Equal-opportunity, demographic, disability, work-authorization, criminal-record,
and security-clearance questions require exact user input and jurisdiction-aware
handling. `Prefer not to answer` is not interchangeable with `No`. Never select
the nearest dropdown option.

U.S. references:

- EEOC prohibited practices: <https://www.eeoc.gov/prohibited-employment-policiespractices>
- EEOC applicant and ADA guidance: <https://www.eeoc.gov/laws/guidance/job-applicants-and-ada>

Local law may differ. Flag possible discrimination for qualified human review;
do not issue a legal conclusion.

## Accommodation

If the user asks for an application or interview accommodation:

1. ask what access barrier and adjustment they want addressed;
2. minimize voluntary disclosure;
3. keep medical details out unless the user deliberately chooses to provide
   them and they are necessary;
4. draft a factual request and route legal questions to a qualified source;
5. do not reduce the user's fit score or opportunity rank.

## Privacy By Default

Apply purpose limitation, minimization, accuracy, retention review, access
control, and transparent sharing. These principles align with GDPR Articles 5,
9, 13, 22, and 25, but implementation alone does not establish compliance.

Before any provider receives personal data, disclose:

- exact fields;
- purpose;
- recipient and jurisdiction if known;
- retention or logging behavior if known;
- whether a lower-data alternative exists.

If the recipient, purpose, jurisdiction, or retention behavior is unknown,
state that explicitly. Do not transmit sensitive data until the recipient and
purpose are independently verified and the user approves the exact disclosure.

Redact unrelated document content. Do not silently upload a complete career
archive to analyze one posting.

## High-Stakes Domains

Refresh and cite current authoritative sources for legal, immigration, tax,
benefits, unemployment, labor-rights, disability, licensing, or financial
questions. The plugin may organize facts and questions but must not replace a
qualified adviser.

If jurisdiction is unknown and materially changes the answer, ask before making
a recommendation. If a deadline is imminent, state the verification gap and
the safest reversible step.

## Well-Being

Offer sustainable pacing, setback planning, recovery, and support-person
check-ins without diagnosing. Avoid shame, false urgency, or application-volume
quotas. If the user signals immediate self-harm or danger, suspend the career
workflow and prioritize locally appropriate emergency or trusted-person help.
