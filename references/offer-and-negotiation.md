# Offer Evaluation And Negotiation

An offer is a written set of terms plus unresolved assumptions. A verbal offer
is a provisional, source-stated signal, not a comparable or accept-ready term
set. Request written terms before comparison, countering, or acceptance, then
normalize them.

## Written Inputs

Collect, when applicable:

- role, level, reporting line, scope, location, start date, and work mode;
- base cash, period, currency, hours, overtime, and pay schedule;
- bonus, commission, targets, guarantees, clawbacks, and payment timing;
- equity instrument, quantity, strike, vesting, cliff, exercise window,
  dilution, liquidity, and tax unknowns;
- benefits, leave, pension, insurance, equipment, learning, relocation, and
  allowances;
- trial period, notice, termination, severance, intellectual property,
  confidentiality, restrictions, repayment, and dispute terms;
- acceptance deadline and who stated it.

The plugin organizes questions; it does not provide legal, tax, investment, or
immigration advice.

## Normalization

Preserve raw terms, then show every formula, currency rate and date, annualized
hours, vesting assumption, and unpriced item. Keep guaranteed and contingent
value separate. Do not assign speculative equity its headline preferred-share
valuation.

Compare nonfinancial dimensions explicitly: work content, manager, autonomy,
learning, stability, flexibility, mission, commute, travel, health impact,
relationships, and option value.

Use `templates/offer-comparison.example.md`.

## Decision Frame

Identify:

- must-haves and dealbreakers;
- preferred tradeoffs;
- current alternatives and their certainty;
- walk-away position, kept private unless disclosed deliberately;
- decision deadline and verification state;
- material jurisdiction-specific questions for a qualified adviser.

An offer score must show weights and uncertainty. Do not let uncertain equity
or a prestigious label dominate a hard constraint.

## Negotiation Assessment

After a real offer, ask whether the user wants to assess negotiation. Examine:

- whether the term is negotiable and who has authority;
- dated market and internal-band evidence when available;
- evidence for level, scope, and impact;
- alternatives and relationship risk;
- user's priorities and risk tolerance;
- channel, timing, and deadline;
- fallback packages that trade across cash, scope, flexibility, start date,
  title, review timing, or benefits.

Do not say everyone must negotiate or promise a gain.

## Counter Plan

Create:

1. appreciation and continued interest, if genuine;
2. prioritized ask with grounded rationale;
3. one or two package alternatives;
4. questions for unresolved terms;
5. private fallback and walk-away logic;
6. exact outbound draft;
7. communication effect plan.

Keep private fallback values out of the message unless the user asks to reveal
them. A verbal agreement is not a final term; request and verify the updated
written offer.

## Acceptance Or Decline

Accepting, declining, or withdrawing is an external communication effect.
Preview the exact recipient, account, terms referenced, message, attachments,
and deadline. After acknowledgement, record a closed pipeline event with the
correct outcome. `hired` requires the defined acceptance or employment outcome,
not merely receipt of an offer.
